## Evidence boundaries

The motor-valve benchmark is a development-feedback/regression example.
Observations influenced later synthetic-zoo design, including actuator headroom;
it is not an untouched independent external test. The published training-data
generator uses synthetic plants. Final NPZ hashes establish file identity, not a
complete causal record of historical training.

The response figures in `README.md` and `docs/STEP4_RESULTS.md` are the project's
own plots of historical measurements. The current discovery animation uses
`make_demo_model` and its public graph/discovery JSON. These renderings do not
assert authorship or redistribution rights over an external model. Dependencies
retain their own licences; this notice records the distribution inventory.

### 证据边界

电机阀门基准属于开发反馈/回归示例。其观察影响了后续合成动物园设计，包括执行器余量，
不能称为从未用于开发的独立外测。公开训练数据生成程序使用合成对象。NPZ最终哈希
证明文件身份，不是历史训练过程的完整因果记录。

`README`与`docs/STEP4_RESULTS.md`中的响应图是本项目对历史测量的自主绘图。
当前发现动图使用`make_demo_model`及公开graph/discovery JSON。上述绘图不主张
外部模型的原创归属或转发权。依赖项保留自身许可，本声明记录分发清单。
